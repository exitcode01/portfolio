_Originally published on my [OpenStreetMap diary](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/Hash_96/diary/408913), 21 June 2026._

Over a weekend (20–21 June), I did intensive fieldwork in the Chitlapakkam and Tambaram areas of Chennai, creating 18 changesets with approximately 96 edits.

## Civic & government infrastructure

Identified and added missing critical facilities. The T13 Police Station and the Tambaram MLA Office — two places people actually need to find — were nowhere on OSM. Also updated the Sembakkam Zone 3 MCC from outdated tags to reflect its current function as a municipal transit yard.

## Fuel & EV infrastructure

Mapped a Shell petrol complex with its petrol station, EV charging station, and convenience store, and removed defunct entries. The EV charger addition is particularly valuable since navigation apps like OsmAnd and Organic Maps source charging data from OpenStreetMap.

## Religious & community spaces

Added two previously unmapped temples in Chitlapakkam, enhanced tags for an existing spiritual centre, and documented the outdoor children's play area in Chitlapakkam Park.

## Health & financial services

Ground surveys revealed missing medical shops, pharmacy counters, and bank branches, which were subsequently added to the map.

## Food & local businesses

Used MapComplete for efficient food venue documentation during neighborhood walks.

## Road & address data quality

Recorded road surface types, captured house numbers from physical signage, and traced building outlines — each contributing to improved address accuracy for delivery and navigation services.
